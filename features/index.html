Two kinds of page

Documents

Fixed, printable pages — A4, A3, A5 or 16:9 slides. The text is measured and paginated so a 50-page study guide prints the way it reads on screen.

Canvas

A free, 1440-wide scene that grows to fit. Turn one idea into a series of up to 25 scenes: a history told as a film storyboard, a science topic as an IKEA manual, a city as a Pokémon card set.
