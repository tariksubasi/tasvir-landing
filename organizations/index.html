Roles

  • Owner: runs the organization, payments and admins.
  • Admin: invites members and manages their roles and credits.
  • Moderator: invites members and gives them credits.
  • Member: creates documents with the credits they are given.

What is in the panel

  • Shared credit pool
  • Bulk credit distribution
  • Excel/CSV import
  • Credit reclaim
  • Credit history
  • Audit log
  • Web and mobile access

Security and privacy

  • Every organization sees only its own members. The organization is resolved from the signed-in session on every request and cannot be changed from outside.
  • Admins see only a member's name, email and role. Members' documents stay theirs.
  • Passwords are never emailed. Invite links are single-use and each person sets their own password.
  • Card payments are taken by Paddle; card details never reach us. Invites, role changes and credit movements are recorded.

Common questions

Can we pay by bank transfer or invoice?

Yes. Email us; after the agreement and invoice we load the credits into your organization pool. With card checkout the credits reach the pool as soon as payment completes.

We are a public school and cannot pay by card. What now?

A card is not required. Pick "bank transfer or invoice" in the form and we prepare a quote and invoice in your organization's name. Once payment arrives we load the credits into your pool.

Can we add students too?

Organization accounts are for teachers and staff; members must be over 18. Teachers can send the documents they make to students with a link that needs no account, or print and hand them out.

How many credits does a document use?

It depends on page count, quality and visuals. An estimate is shown before generation starts, and the free trial lets you measure it on your own topic.

What happens to credits when someone leaves?

Their unused organization credit returns to the pool. Accounts the organization created are closed when they are removed.

Can members use the mobile app?

Yes. Members sign in to the iOS and Android app with the same account and create with organization credit. The organization panel itself is on the web at app.tasvir.ai.

What is the refund policy?

Organization packs are non-refundable once added to the pool; an approved refund covers only credit still unused in the pool. See the Billing & Refund Policy.
